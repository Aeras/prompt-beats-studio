## ru.bylina — epic vocal prosody and melodic variants
**Source C**: Alexander M. Petrov, “Is there a three-ictic taktovik in the verse of Russian bylinas?”, *Voprosy Jazykoznanija*, 2023 issue 4, pp. 65–95. DOI https://doi.org/10.31857/0373-658X.2023.4.65-95 ; publisher abstract at https://vja.ruslang.ru/en/archive/2023-4/65-95 .
- Reports a corpus of 10,111 lines / 41 published texts plus 632 recorded lines / 36 fragments; argues that 3- and 4-stress lines alternate and warns against inferring actual sung prosody only from text detached from melody. The article's proposal **must not** be generalized to all bylina traditions without examining the study and variants.
**Source D**: Galina V. Lobkova, “Russian Epic Tunes in 1871 Manuscript”, *Opera Musicologica* 15(4), 2023, pp. 118–142.
