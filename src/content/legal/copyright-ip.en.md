# PROMPTBEATS COPYRIGHT / INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY POLICY

**Last updated:** 21 September 2026

## 1. Purpose of this Policy

This Copyright / Intellectual Property Policy explains the rules that apply on PromptBeats to copyright, related rights and other associated intellectual-property rights.

It covers, among other things, user uploads, audio files, lyrics, AI-generated and AI-assisted content, covers and images, public prompts, downloads, reports, formal Copyright / IP Notices, moderation cases, creator communication, appeals, repeat infringement and related matters.

This Policy should be read together with the PromptBeats Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

PromptBeats currently operates as an independent, personal and non-commercial project. That status does not limit obligations that apply mandatorily under applicable law.

## 2. Core Principle

Users must upload, publish, share or otherwise use through PromptBeats only content for which they have the necessary rights, licenses, permissions or other lawful basis.

The technical ability to upload, store, publish or share a file does not constitute confirmation by PromptBeats that the user holds the rights required for that use.

PromptBeats does not conduct a general advance copyright-clearance review of every upload and does not certify ownership, originality or non-infringement.

## 3. User Content and Ownership

Users retain the rights they lawfully hold in their own content.

Use of PromptBeats does not transfer ownership of a user’s works to PromptBeats.

The limited license required for hosting, technical processing, display, sharing in accordance with user settings, security and moderation is described in the Terms of Use. That license does not transfer ownership to PromptBeats.

PromptBeats does not acquire the right to present a user-owned musical work as its own.

## 4. AI-Generated and AI-Assisted Content

Creating content with AI assistance does not automatically mean that the resulting material is fully protected by copyright, that the user owns exclusive rights in every element, that the output is unique, that nobody else can obtain a similar result, or that no third-party rights are involved.

The existence, scope and ownership of rights depend on applicable law, the extent of human creative contribution, the source of any material used, the terms of third-party AI providers and the facts of each case.

PromptBeats does not provide legal certification of originality, copyrightability or non-infringement.

Where supported by the platform, users may upload content that was created or processed through an external service.

Users are solely responsible for confirming whether the terms of that service allow them to own or use the output, publish it, distribute it, use it commercially, upload it to a third-party platform or grant rights in it to others.

Unless expressly stated otherwise, PromptBeats is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting as an official partner or representative of Suno, Udio or any other third-party service.

## 6. Uploads Prohibited on Rights Grounds

Users must not upload or publish content where they know, or reasonably should know, that the content:
- infringes copyright,
- infringes related rights,
- contains an unauthorized sound recording,
- contains an uncleared sample where permission is required,
- infringes the rights of an author, performer or producer,
- infringes a trademark or other intellectual-property right,
- uses cover art or an image without the necessary rights,
- or breaches contractual restrictions imposed by a third-party service.

## 7. Covers, Artwork, Logos and Images

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have the required rights in any cover art, photographs, logos or other images they upload.

Protected artwork, photographs or third-party logos must not be used without the required right, license, permission or other lawful basis.

## 8. Public Prompts

Where PromptBeats allows a user to make a prompt public and viewable or copyable by others, the user should understand that the prompt may be viewed, copied, adapted and used by other users in accordance with the relevant platform functionality.

Publication of a prompt does not, by itself, confirm that the prompt is protected by exclusive copyright.

If a user wants a prompt to remain confidential, they should not publish it through a feature that makes the prompt public or copyable.

## 9. Downloads from PromptBeats

The availability of a track for download does not, by itself, transfer copyright, transfer ownership, grant a commercial license, authorize derivative works or constitute a waiver of the rights holder’s rights.

Anyone who downloads content is responsible for ensuring that any subsequent use is lawful and permitted by the rights holder.

## 10. In-App Reports and Formal Copyright / IP Notices

PromptBeats provides a general **Report** mechanism for in-platform moderation reports, including reports concerning possible copyright or other intellectual-property issues.

Using the general Report mechanism does not automatically mean that the submission constitutes a formal legal notice.

Formal notices concerning possible infringement of copyright or other intellectual-property rights may be submitted through:

**Email:** support@promptbeats.app  
**Contact form:** https://promptbeats.app/contact

A formal Copyright / IP Notice may also be submitted by a person or entity that does not have a PromptBeats account, to the extent required or permitted by the applicable procedure.

For a communication to be handled as a formal notice, it must contain enough information to identify the relevant content and assess the claim in accordance with Section 11.

## 11. Information Required in a Formal Copyright / IP Notice

To allow a formal Copyright / IP report to be assessed effectively, it should, to the extent required or reasonably necessary, include:

1. a sufficiently reasoned explanation of why the identified content is considered unlawful or infringing,
2. a clear identification of the protected work, right or other legally protected interest,
3. the precise electronic location of the reported content, such as a specific URL, track link or another sufficiently precise identifier,
4. the name of the person or entity submitting the notice, where required,
5. a valid email address or other appropriate electronic contact information,
6. where applicable, information showing that the reporter is the rights holder or an authorized representative,
7. a statement of good-faith belief that the information and allegations in the report are accurate and complete to the best of the reporter’s knowledge,
8. any additional information reasonably necessary to assess the specific matter.

PromptBeats may request supplementary information where the original submission does not allow an adequate and informed assessment.

Providing the above information does not mean that the claim is automatically accepted or that the content will be removed.

## 12. Receipt and Processing of a Notice

Where applicable law requires acknowledgment of receipt and the reporter has provided valid electronic contact details, PromptBeats will provide the required acknowledgment through an available communication channel.

Formal notices are assessed in a timely, diligent, objective and non-arbitrary manner to the extent required by applicable law and having regard to the facts of the particular case.

Submitting a notice does not automatically mean that the claim is valid or that the content will be removed.

Where required, the reporter will be informed of the decision concerning the relevant content and of any available avenues for review or other legal redress.

## 13. What Happens After a Report

Depending on the circumstances, PromptBeats may:
- assess the information in the report,
- request additional information from the reporter,
- examine the reported content,
- open a moderation case,
- contact the uploader or creator,
- request an explanation, proof of rights, a license or other relevant evidence,
- temporarily restrict access to the content where necessary and lawful,
- remove or restrict content where there is a sufficient basis,
- take no action where there is insufficient basis,
- or reject a report that is manifestly insufficient, abusive or unfounded.

A temporary restriction does not, by itself, amount to a final legal determination of ownership or infringement.

The outcome depends on the available information, applicable law, the rights of the parties involved, this Copyright / Intellectual Property Policy and the Terms of Use.

If the creator does not respond to a reasonable request for information or proof of rights, the matter may be assessed on the basis of the information available at that time, without automatically shifting to the creator any burden of proof that does not arise under applicable law.

## 14. Notice to the Person Who Submitted a Formal Notice

Where required by applicable law and where suitable contact details are available, PromptBeats will inform the reporting party of the decision made in relation to the specific content.

Where required, the notice may include information about available means of challenge or legal redress.

This does not mean that the reporter is entitled to access the uploader’s private data or communications.

## 15. Notice to the Uploader / Creator

Where action is taken against content because of a copyright or other IP claim, the affected user will receive the information and reasons required by applicable law and appropriate to the circumstances.

Where required, the notice may include:
- the type of restriction and, where relevant, its duration or scope,
- the principal facts and information on which the decision was based,
- the relevant legal or contractual ground,
- information regarding any automated means used, where disclosure is required,
- and available avenues for challenge or review.

Information may be limited where permitted by law, particularly for reasons of security, protection of third parties or avoidance of interference with a lawful investigation.

The reporter’s identity or contact details are not automatically disclosed to the uploader through the ordinary moderation process.

Specific reporter information may be disclosed only where required or permitted by applicable law and where necessary for the relevant procedure.

## 16. Moderation Cases and Communication with the Creator

Where a report requires further review, PromptBeats may open a moderation case linked to the reported content.

The case is available to authorized moderation roles in accordance with their permissions. When an authorized moderation user takes responsibility for the case, that person is designated as the assigned handler.

Other authorized moderation users may read the case in accordance with their role permissions, while creator-facing communication in an active case is ordinarily carried out by the assigned handler. A Super Admin may intervene in any case in accordance with their elevated permissions.

The assigned handler may request information, clarification, proof of rights, a license or other relevant evidence from the creator. The creator may respond within the active case and provide information, explanations, links or other relevant observations.

Moderation-case communication is not a general user-to-user private-messaging feature and does not allow users to open arbitrary conversations with moderators.

The reporter’s identity is not disclosed to the creator through the ordinary case-communication process.

## 17. Case Ownership and Internal Access

Assignment of a moderation case is intended to avoid simultaneous or conflicting responses from different reviewers.

While a case is actively handled:
- authorized moderation roles may have read access in accordance with their permissions,
- the assigned handler has the ordinary write access for creator-facing communication,
- a Super Admin may intervene regardless of assignment,
- any reassignment or change of handler is recorded in the audit trail.

Internal moderation notes and other internal case information are not shown to the creator.

## 18. Initial Moderation Decision

After reviewing the case and the available information, an initial moderation decision may be issued.

Where the initial review is carried out by a Moderator or Senior Moderator, the initial moderation decision is approved at Admin level in accordance with the platform’s current workflow.

Super Admin approval is not required for an ordinary initial moderation decision.

After the initial decision is issued, the creator is informed through the case or another relevant platform mechanism. Active communication may be closed or restricted after the decision, subject to any available appeal process.

## 19. Appeal / Request for Review

Where the decision is appealable, a creator who believes that the moderation decision is incorrect may use the available **Appeal / Request Review** function.

The creator may explain why they believe they hold the relevant right or license, why they believe there is no infringement, why they believe the original report or decision was incorrect or incomplete, or what additional information should be considered.

The appeal remains linked to the original moderation case and does not create a separate general conversation.

## 20. Appeal Review and Final Approval

Appeals are reviewed at Admin level in accordance with the current workflow.

The Admin re-examines the report, case history, creator responses, initial decision and any new information submitted with the appeal.

The outcome of the Admin re-review is not final until final Super Admin approval has been completed.

Following an appeal, final approval is performed by a Super Admin. In accordance with the current workflow, the Super Admin may confirm the proposed outcome, reject it, modify it where permitted by their role permissions, or return the case for further review.

After the Super Admin’s final action, the creator is informed of the final decision and the case is closed, unless an exceptional administrative reopening takes place.

The availability of an internal review process does not limit any rights or remedies provided by applicable law.

## 21. Audit Trail and Case Integrity

PromptBeats may record audit events relating to report creation, case assignment, reassignment, creator communication, moderation actions, decisions, appeals, Admin review, Super Admin final approval and case closure.

These records are intended to preserve the integrity of the moderation process, support security, handle disputes and comply with applicable obligations.

Creator-visible messages, internal notes and decision history are distinct and should not be treated as interchangeable.

## 22. Repeat Infringement

PromptBeats may take stronger measures in cases involving serious or repeated infringement of intellectual-property rights.

Depending on the circumstances, such measures may include content removal, restriction of upload privileges, temporary suspension or account bans.

The existence of an earlier report does not, by itself, establish that an infringement occurred.

When determining whether conduct constitutes repeat infringement, PromptBeats takes account of actual decisions and the evidence in each matter, rather than merely the number of reports submitted.

Measures should be based on an assessment of the facts and, where required, be appropriate and proportionate.

## 23. Bad-Faith or False Reports

Knowingly submitting false, misleading or abusive copyright reports is prohibited.

Abuse of the reporting mechanism may lead to restrictions on reporting privileges or other appropriate measures under the Terms of Use and applicable law.

## 24. No General Legal Determination

PromptBeats does not act as a court and does not issue binding legal determinations of ownership, authorship or copyright.

In complex or serious disputes, the parties may need to seek advice from a lawyer or apply to a court or other competent authority.

PromptBeats may comply with valid court judgments, orders or other lawful requests issued by competent authorities.

## 25. Trademarks and Other Rights

This Policy primarily concerns copyright and related rights, but PromptBeats may also review reports involving trademarks, rights-infringing impersonation, image or personality rights and other forms of intellectual or related property where such matters fall within the platform’s functionality and obligations.
