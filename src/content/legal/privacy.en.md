## 1. Purpose of this Policy

This Privacy & Personal Data Policy explains how PromptBeats collects, uses, stores, transfers and protects personal data when users interact with the platform and its features.

PromptBeats currently operates as an **independent, personal and non-commercial project**, developed and maintained on a voluntary basis during the creator’s and operator’s available free time.

At the time this Policy applies, the platform’s standard user-facing features are provided free of charge. PromptBeats’ current free and non-commercial model is not a commitment that the platform will remain non-commercial or free indefinitely.

Any voluntary financial support for the project is provided through independent third-party providers and does not constitute a purchase of services, subscription, or consideration for additional features or special privileges.

This Policy should be read together with the PromptBeats Terms of Use, Cookie Policy and Copyright / Intellectual Property Policy.

## 5. Categories of Personal Data We May Process

Depending on how you use the platform, we may process the following categories of personal data.

### 5.1 Account and identification data

This may include email address, user ID, username or display name, authentication-related information, account creation date, account status, role or access permissions where relevant, and technical information required for secure sign-in.

Passwords are not intended to be stored by PromptBeats in readable form.

### 5.2 Public profile data

Where a user chooses to provide or publish them, we may process display name, username, avatar or profile image, bio, public tracks, playlists, follower/following information, likes or other publicly displayed engagement data, and other information the user chooses to make visible on their public profile.

Information a user publishes publicly may be visible to other users or visitors.

### 5.3 Creative content

This may include lyrics, prompts, song concepts, song titles, musical style, mood, BPM, instrumentation, song structure, production directions, song blueprints, revisions, Quality Check inputs and outputs, saved projects and other creative material submitted or created through the service.

### 5.4 Uploaded tracks and images

Where the feature is available, we may process audio files, track metadata, titles and descriptions, cover artwork, visibility settings, download settings, explicit-content labels, public/share links and other information associated with an uploaded track.

### 5.5 AI feature usage data

When a user uses AI functionality, we may process the user’s prompt or other input, generation parameters, selected language, selected model where applicable, AI-generated output, Quality Check input/output, request status, error information, timestamps, usage counters, rate-limit information and technical metadata necessary to execute or diagnose the request.

### 5.6 Social and engagement data

This may include likes, favorites, playlists, follows, notifications, interaction history necessary for those features to function, and related timestamps.

### 5.7 Reports

When a report is submitted, we may process:
- reporter user ID,
- the reported track or other content ID,
- reason for the report,
- additional description,
- supporting information supplied by the reporter,
- timestamp,
- report status,
- and data needed for anti-abuse or duplicate-detection purposes.

The reporter’s identity is not ordinarily disclosed to the owner of the reported content.

Moderators and Senior Moderators do not receive reporter identity from the backend, while Admin-level users may have access where necessary and authorized.

### 5.8 Moderation cases and case messages

When a report becomes a moderation case, we may process:
- case ID,
- report reference,
- target track/content,
- creator/owner ID,
- assigned handler,
- assignment/reassignment history,
- case status,
- creator-visible messages,
- creator replies,
- structured moderation requests,
- internal moderation notes,
- system events,
- read/unread state,
- timestamps,
- and related moderation actions.

Creator-facing communication is tied to the specific case and is not a general private-messaging feature.

### 5.9 Moderation decisions and appeals

We may process:
- initial findings,
- recommendation,
- Admin initial decision,
- reasons for the decision,
- moderation action,
- creator notification,
- appeal text,
- appeal submission timestamp,
- Admin appeal review,
- proposed appeal outcome,
- Super Admin final review,
- final outcome,
- final closure timestamp,
- and audit history.

### 5.10 Copyright / legal notice data

This may include:
- name of the reporter/notifier,
- email address or other contact details,
- status as rights holder or representative,
- description of the protected work,
- URLs or identifiers,
- supporting information,
- good-faith statements,
- correspondence,
- moderation outcome,
- dispute/appeal history,
- and information needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

### 5.11 Technical and security data

This may include IP address, device/browser information, session information, authentication events, request logs, error logs, security events, timestamps, rate-limit events and other technical data necessary for operation, security and troubleshooting.

### 5.12 Communications with PromptBeats

If you contact us, we may process your email address or other contact details, message content, attachments and related communication history.

### 5.13 Voluntary financial support through third-party providers

If a user chooses to support the project financially through an independent third-party provider such as PayPal, Buy Me a Coffee, Revolut or a similar service, the payment or transfer is completed through that provider’s systems.

## 14. Special Access Rights Within the Moderation System

The active moderation workflow applies specific access restrictions.

### Reports

Reports are visible to authorized moderation roles.

Reporter identity:
- is not provided to a Moderator,
- is not provided to a Senior Moderator,
- may be available to Admin-level users where necessary and authorized.

### Moderation cases

Once a case has been taken over:
- authorized moderation roles may read it,
- the assigned handler has the ordinary write access for creator-facing communication,
- a Super Admin may intervene regardless of assignment,
- reassignment and other critical actions are recorded in the audit trail.

### Appeals

Review is carried out at Admin level.

Following an appeal, final approval of the decision is performed by a Super Admin in accordance with the current workflow.

## 15. Moderation Case Communication

PromptBeats provides case-based moderation communication for specific moderation matters.

Case data may include report metadata, case status, assigned handler, assignment/reassignment history, creator-visible messages, creator replies, structured moderation requests, internal notes, system events, decision history, appeal text, appeal review, final approval events, read/unread state, timestamps and audit data.

Creator-facing communication exists only within a specific moderation case and is not a general user-to-user messaging feature.

While a case is active, the creator may respond within that specific case in accordance with the case status.

After a decision has been issued or the case has been finally closed, reply functionality may be disabled while the historical record remains available in accordance with access rights and the retention policy.

## 16. Appeals and Final Review

Where a right of appeal is available in relation to a moderation decision, PromptBeats processes the data necessary for that review.

An appeal may include the appeal text, additional explanations or evidence from the creator, the original report and case history, the initial moderation decision, Admin review, proposed appeal outcome, Super Admin final approval and final closure event.

The final outcome following an appeal is communicated to the creator through the relevant moderation mechanism.

## 17. Audit Logs and Moderation History

PromptBeats may retain audit logs relating to report creation, case assignment, case reassignment, creator communication, moderation actions, initial decisions, appeals, Admin review, Super Admin final approval, final closure and security-sensitive administrative actions.

Audit logs are used to preserve process integrity, support security, prevent abuse, handle disputes and comply with applicable obligations.

They should not contain more personal data than is necessary for those purposes.

## 18. Retention — How Long We Keep Personal Data

Personal data are retained only for as long as necessary or justified for the purposes for which they are processed, in accordance with the principle of storage limitation.

Where it is not practical to set a single fixed period for every category, retention is determined by criteria such as:
- whether the account or relevant content remains active,
- whether the data are still required to provide a feature requested by the user,
- the duration and status of a moderation case, appeal, report or dispute,
- security, anti-abuse, fraud-prevention and troubleshooting needs,
- the existence of a legal retention obligation,
- the need to establish, exercise or defend legal claims,
- and the technically necessary lifecycle of backups or logs.

More specifically:
- account and profile data are generally retained while the account remains active and for as long thereafter as necessary for the lawful purposes described above,
- uploaded content and projects are retained while they remain stored by the user or until deleted, subject to backups, moderation, disputes or legal obligations,
- AI request metadata, error/security logs and anti-abuse records are retained for a limited period appropriate to the relevant technical, operational and security need,
- reports, moderation cases, case messages, internal notes, decisions, appeals and audit events may be retained for as long as necessary to preserve the integrity of the moderation process, prevent abuse, handle disputes and comply with legal obligations,
- copyright / legal notices and related correspondence may be retained for as long as necessary to manage the matter and any related legal claims,
- information concerning voluntary financial support received from a third-party provider is retained only for as long as necessary for administration, accounting/tax compliance where applicable, security or dispute resolution,
- backups may retain deleted data for a limited period until they are overwritten as part of the normal backup lifecycle.

When the purpose for retention no longer applies and there is no other lawful basis, the data are deleted or anonymized as appropriate.
